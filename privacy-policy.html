Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

"On This Day Videos" is a personal automation project operated by an individual developer (the "operator") to generate and publish short-form educational history videos to social media accounts that the operator personally owns and controls (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram).

What this application does

The application fetches publicly available historical event summaries from Wikipedia, generates a narrated video using AI text and speech generation, and publishes the resulting video to the operator's own social media accounts using each platform's official API.

Data collection

This application does not collect, store, process, or share personal data belonging to any third party or end user. It is not a public-facing service, has no user accounts, login system, or public API of its own, and does not serve or interact with any users other than the operator.

Third-party platform access

The application connects to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram solely via each platform's official OAuth authorization flow, using access explicitly granted by the operator to their own accounts on those platforms, for the sole purpose of uploading video content the application itself generates.
