Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026

"On This Day Videos" is a personal automation project, not a public product or service. It has no public users, no sign-up process, and no functionality accessible to anyone other than its operator.

Scope

This application exists solely to automate the creation and publishing of short-form history videos to social media accounts owned by its operator. It integrates with third-party platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) strictly through their official developer APIs and in accordance with each platform's own terms of service and developer policies.

Content

Video content is generated using publicly available Wikipedia content plus AI-generated narration, images, and text. Content is reviewed by the operator before being made public on any platform.
